How do you identify and mitigate bias in your AI system's training data and algorithms?

Context: Bias in AI systems can lead to unfair treatment of individuals or groups. This question examines your approach to identifying potential biases in training data and implementing measures to mitigate them throughout the AI development lifecycle.

Regularly audit datasets for imbalances or biases and use tools like Fairlearn to detect and mitigate issues.

Implement fairness constraints during model training and review model outcomes for biases before deployment.
