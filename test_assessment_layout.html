FA-3 Fairness & Bias

How does your organization ensure that AI systems are designed and deployed in a way that promotes fairness and avoids bias?

Context: Bias in AI systems can lead to unfair treatment of individuals or groups. This question assesses your organization's approach to identifying, measuring, and mitigating bias in AI systems throughout their lifecycle.

Ideal (3 points)
Regularly audit datasets for imbalances or biases and use tools like Fairlearn to detect and mitigate issues.
Good (2 points)
Implement fairness constraints during model training and review model outcomes for biases before deployment.
Basic (1 point)
Conduct ad hoc reviews of the system to check for fairness concerns.

No specific measures have been implemented yet.
