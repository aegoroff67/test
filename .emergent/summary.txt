<product_requirements>
The AM AI SAFE application is a full-stack platform designed to help organizations evaluate their AI maturity through a suite of specialized assessments. The core functionality is to guide users through questionnaires, process their answers, and generate insightful summary pages and downloadable DOCX/PDF reports.

The platform was expanded from a single AI System Maturity Assessment MVP to include three additional types:
1.  **AI Awareness & Foundations Assessment:** A free, entry-level assessment.
2.  **AI Readiness Assessment:** A premium, more in-depth assessment.
3.  **Organisation-wide AI Maturity Assessment:** A premium, comprehensive assessment.

Key features implemented include:
*   **Dynamic Assessment Framework:** The backend dynamically serves questions, scoring logic, and progress data based on the selected . The frontend renders corresponding UI themes, response options, and results pages.
*   **Onboarding Forms:** Each assessment type has a unique, color-coded pre-assessment onboarding form to collect tailored metadata.
*   **Results Pages:** A results summary page has been implemented for both the System and Awareness assessments, featuring heatmaps, domain scores, and report download buttons.
*   **Content Management:** The content on the public-facing authentication page has been updated to reflect the new multi-assessment suite.
*   **Paywall Architecture:** A detailed architectural plan for implementing a Stripe-based paywall for premium assessments has been outlined.
</product_requirements>
